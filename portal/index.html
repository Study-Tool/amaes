HOW IT WORKS

One student answers. Everyone gets the answer.

Instead of every student searching for the exact same questions over and over, AMAES Toolkit connects everyone on Moodle to a shared answer pool. When anyone finishes and reviews a quiz, verified answers are saved anonymously and added to the daily public archive.

Shared Answer Pool

Answers are saved across quizzes. If another student has already solved the question, the verified answer appears for you immediately.

100% Anonymous Sharing

When you review a completed quiz, verified checkmarks are contributed automatically. Zero personal names, student IDs, or logins are ever collected.

Autonomous Background Pilot

Runs hands-free across questions and pages. Automatically picks verified answers, resolves fallback study guides, and advances cleanly.

Built-in Google Gemini AI

Direct in-quiz AI solving for uncertain questions with in-session caching, elimination guards against known wrong choices, and configurable retries.

Online Study Guides

Integrates student study guides (AMAUOED & Jenny's Online) directly into the Study Library with live course match indicators and fallback auto-picking.

1-Click Solving & Shortcuts

Auto-highlights correct choices on Moodle, with instant shortcuts like C to copy for AI, V to paste AI answers, and 1-click lecture completion.
